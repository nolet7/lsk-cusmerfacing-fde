# CTO mandate and project charter
**Fictional client:** Meridian Commercial Services, a B2B software distributor with Salesforce-like CRM, ERP orders, a helpdesk and spreadsheet-based analytics. This is a simulated customer engagement, not a claim of real client delivery.

**Problem:** Sales and customer-success teams spend time reconciling customer health, orders and case data across siloed systems; prospects cannot evaluate the SaaS platform without a custom integration.

**Outcomes:** (1) Deliver working legacy SaaS workflows before AI. (2) Prove scoped SQL/REST data integration. (3) Show a grounded, read-only LLM customer account briefing. (4) Create repeatable onboarding and demos. (5) Transfer ownership and support after a monitored pilot.

**Stakeholders:** CTO sponsor, VP Sales, enterprise architecture, security, data owners, customer-success lead, product manager and FDE delivery lead.

**Constraints:** No real PII; tenant isolation; no write actions by AI; versioned APIs; auditable tool usage; optional LLM provider; consent for transcript storage; controlled spend.

**Non-goals:** Autonomous refunds, billing changes, outbound email, unapproved SaaS connectors, claiming SOC 2 compliance or certified production readiness.

**Decision gates:** Discovery signoff -> POC acceptance -> pilot change approval -> production readiness signoff.
